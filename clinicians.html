03 For clinicians

What arrives on your desk.

One page, always the same order
Concerns, chronology, investigations, family history, the patient’s questions.
Clinical terms with sources
Standard phenotype terms, each linked to the patient’s own words or a document.
Gaps marked
Missing dates and unread documents are flagged, never made up.

Stage two: decision support.

A GP uploads a structured history. The system suggests rare conditions worth considering and the tests that could confirm or exclude them. The clinician decides.

  • Developed as a medical device under the EU MDR
  • Quality system to ISO 13485
  • Risk management to ISO 14971
  • Software lifecycle to IEC 62304

How we get there.
