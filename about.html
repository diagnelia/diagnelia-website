06 About

Why we started.

People with rare conditions often see doctor after doctor for years. The pattern only shows when someone sees all of it.

We are building that someone: an AI that holds the whole story for the next doctor.

Who is building it.

What comes next.

  1. Pilot

    A first test with 30 to 50 people in one language, for two symptom groups such as muscle weakness and joint pain. The goal: their doctors say the summary helped at the appointment.

  2. Launch

    English, German and Ukrainian, more symptom groups, and the Plus plan.

  3. Clinician tool

    Decision support, validated and certified under the EU MDR.
