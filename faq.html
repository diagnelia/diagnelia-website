07 Questions

For patients and families

Will Diagnelia tell me what disease I have?

No. It organises your history and helps you find the right next step. Only a doctor can make a diagnosis, and guessing does more harm than good.

Who is it for?

Adults searching for a diagnosis, and parents or partners helping them. If you have symptoms that years of visits have not explained, it is for you.

Do I need my medical records?

No. Your memory is enough to start. Documents make the history more complete, and you can add them later.

Which languages?

The pilot runs in one language. English, German and Ukrainian are planned for launch.

Safety and data

Is Diagnelia a medical device?

The patient tool organises information and does not diagnose, so it is not a medical device. The clinician tool will be developed and certified as one under the EU MDR.

Where is my data stored?

On servers in the European Union, encrypted in transit and at rest, under the GDPR.

Is my story used to train AI?

Only if you opt in. You can change your mind at any time.

What if I describe something urgent?

The AI stops and tells you to call 112 or see a doctor today.

Clinicians and partners

How do I read a patient’s chronology?

Open the PDF or the private link your patient shares. No account needed.

How can my organisation take part?

Write to us about a pilot. We start with one symptom group and 30 to 50 people.

Do you take money from pharmaceutical companies?

Only for early detection programmes, under published rules: no patient access, no promotion, every funder named.
