Privacy, in plain words.

A summary. The full policy is published before launch.

09 Privacy

1 Who is responsible

Diagnelia, reachable at ceo@diagnelia.com. Company details are in the legal notice.

2 What we collect

Your answers, your documents, your account details and the technical data the service needs.

3 Why

To build your history and next steps. Health data only with your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)).

4 Where

Servers in the European Union, encrypted in transit and at rest.

5 AI training

Never with your data, unless you opt in. You can withdraw at any time.

6 Sharing

Only when you share it yourself. No selling, no advertising.

7 Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, export, objection, and a complaint to your data authority.

8 This website

No tracking cookies, no analytics, no third-party fonts.
